# Product Steering Document

## Vision
To build a highly secure, privacy-first, community-driven stray cat rescue and management platform that aids volunteers, shelters, and organizers.

## Key Directives
- **Zero-Knowledge Logs**: Encrypted client-side user databases and private configurations.
- **Fuzz Location**: Snapped PostGIS coordinate matrices for stray cat colony protection.
- **TNR Event Coordination**: Transparent scheduling and campaign statistics.
